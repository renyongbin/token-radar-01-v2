How the implied value is computed

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × age factor × chain factor.

  • Category multiple: the median market-cap / TVL of the protocols in the same DefiLlama category whose token already trades. A category with fewer than three traded comps uses the global median instead. Multiples are clamped to 0.03–2.00.
  • Growth factor: 1 + half the 7-day TVL change, clamped to 0.75–1.50. No reported change counts as flat.
  • Age factor: 1 + 0.12 per year the protocol has run with no token, capped at four years. A protocol DefiLlama does not date counts as one year old.
  • Chain factor: 1 + 0.02 per chain the protocol runs on, capped at eight chains.

The board ranks the 180 pre-token protocols with the highest implied value, and always includes all 40 of the snapshot's watchlist. Protocols whose token already trades are not ranked here: their tokens have a market price already, so there is nothing to imply. Neither are wrapped, staked or bridged products of a token that already trades — a wrapped bitcoin, a staked-ETH product or a bridge is not a protocol still waiting on a launch of its own — nor versioned builds of a protocol: of the snapshot's pre-token protocols are set aside on that rule.
